## Limits

- Matching recall is low: 22 of 159 headword entries matched. The canonical article is often absent from the BM25 pool, and a reranker cannot recover a missing candidate. Treatise pages (SHIP-BUILDING, MAGNETISM) carry a running header but no headword, so their query is the running header plus body text.
- The plate match `e1845-01` (text `XVII.`) is accepted by the rule but is not a real association. The image rerank did not prevent it. It stays in the results as a known failure.
- Quotes are checked against saved text, not against the scan image. OCR errors inside quotes are preserved by design. The checks confirm ids, quotes, and the model reply; they do not confirm the facts in an answer.
- The term comparison measures vocabulary, not factual agreement. OCR noise inflates Britannica-only terms.
- The Super VL model tends to normalize OCR noise inside quotes unless the prompt forbids it. Three of the first ten runs failed the quote check for this reason.
- The agent stage runs its own tool loop through `ChatClient`. Hermes runs the same tools interactively; Hermes chat sessions were not recorded as run records.
